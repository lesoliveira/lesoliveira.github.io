Research

My research is directed towards pattern recognition and machine learning. These are the topics my recent and long-running work concentrates on:

  • Handwriting recognition and document analysis
  • Signature and writer verification
  • Dynamic selection of classifiers and regressors
  • Ensemble learning and pool generation
  • Dissimilarity representation and metric learning
  • Open-set recognition
  • Concept drift and data streams
  • Texture classification: forest species, corals, histopathology
  • Deep learning for medical images
  • Forecasting and nowcasting: wind, lightning, precipitation
  • Fault detection in power transmission lines
  • Machine learning for industrial processes
  • Language models for health data and text-to-SQL
  • Machine learning security
