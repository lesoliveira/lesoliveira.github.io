#DeOlhoNosCorais project is in the media.

#DeOlhoNosCorais

The Bori agency published an article about the work of master’s student Daniel Pimenta. Daniel worked on the #DeOlhoNosCorais project, which aims to classify coral images using deep learning. The project was developed in partnership with Professor Guilherme Longo from UFRN and had the support of the Serra Pilheira Institute. If you are interested in this project, check out this article published in PeerJ.
